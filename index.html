QUESTIONS

Good to know.

How does the coaching work?+

Get personalized feedback on your gameplay, identify your biggest mistakes, and receive clear goals to focus on in your next games.

What can a coach help me improve?+

We focus on the areas that matter most for climbing — mechanics, macro, decision-making, champion pool, laning, and overall game consistency.

Is coaching worth it if I'm stuck in the same rank?+

Yes. A coach helps you understand why you're stuck and gives you a structured plan to break through your current rank.
